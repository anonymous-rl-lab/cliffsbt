# Claim boundaries

## Licensed

- Operational risk cliffs occur repeatedly in the balanced Covertype elevation stream.
- Fine-scale risk differences can be unreliable even when risk levels are reliable.
- Model-relative signed margin transport is more informative than unsigned training
  distance in the final fresh holdout.
- The true trained boundary outperforms coordinate-permuted boundary placebos.
- Robust median equal-quantile margin transport is a strong candidate state variable.
- The mechanism evidence is distribution-level and model-conditional.

## Not licensed

- Universal or exact first/second-order risk geometry.
- A universal 45 m physical constant.
- Literal longitudinal sample flow or synchronized sample crossings.
- Causal proof that robust median transport generates every Cliff.
- Strict robust-median superiority over mean transport across all source/seed clusters.
- Unlabeled deployment use of the signed-margin statistic.
- A complete formal pass: the terminal fresh holdout is 9/10 gates and STOP.

## Required wording discipline

Use `supports`, `is consistent with`, or `provides external distribution-level
evidence`. Do not use `proves`, `establishes sample synchrony`, `universally`, or
`all gates passed`.
