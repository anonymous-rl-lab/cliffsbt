# Unified claims and limits

## Licensed by the combined artifact

1. A generalization Cliff is operationally defined as crossing a task-risk
   boundary; ordinary distribution drift alone is neither necessary nor
   sufficient terminology for the event.
2. In the balanced Covertype protocol, robust signed transport of probability
   mass in the trained model's margin space tracks risk change better than
   unsigned displacement alone. This is distribution-level and
   model-conditional evidence.
3. In the controlled paired TorchSig system, risk increments are realized by
   net decision-boundary crossings. The observed first crossings are mostly
   persistent and temporally distributed, not a required synchronized pulse.
4. The TorchSig effect is largely specific to the trained model's true boundary
   relative to frozen random-boundary placebos.
5. Within the frozen TorchSig simulator and ExtraTrees family, changing
   training support changes `(b,H,Q)`, later risk, operational-Cliff crossing,
   and paired incident crossing on common latent deployment streams.
6. The two fields jointly support the manuscript's mechanistic chain while
   contributing different evidence levels: external distribution-level
   falsification in Covertype and controlled sample-level confirmation in
   TorchSig.
7. In the frozen five-seed TorchSig Round 12C pilot, a 5% repair set covering
   all 16 queried class-by-path-by-phase cells reduces terminal risk more than
   a same-size set concentrated on the largest local-hazard scores. The paired
   advantage is 0.09414 with a training-seed-clustered 95% interval
   [0.03633, 0.14648].
8. Once all 16 cells are covered, adding local-hazard ranking contributes only
   0.00039 mean terminal-risk reduction in this pilot, with a post hoc interval
   spanning zero. Local hazard remains a mechanism measurement, but is not a
   sufficient small-budget set-selection objective here.
9. Official CIFAR-10-C Round 13D confirms exact paired true-boundary transport
   in a second neural-network benchmark: all 15 corruption families increase
   endpoint risk for all three seeds, but only 11 exhaust headroom for all
   seeds. Degradation and positive risk drift therefore do not automatically
   constitute a Cliff.
10. Round 13D shows that incident flow must be offset by recovery flow. Large
    recovery can prevent headroom exhaustion, and an incorrect pseudo-boundary
    has materially worse accounting reconstruction than the trained task
    boundary.
11. Round 13E establishes repair efficacy in the image domain: coverage reduces
    endpoint error relative to the clean-only baseline by 0.04741 and relative
    to random augmentation by 0.00763 under the frozen equal-budget design.
12. Round 13E prospectively rejects the stronger coverage-only allocation law.
    Hazard concentration has lower endpoint error, risk area, crossing
    fraction, and endpoint net flux even though both dangerous arms cover all
    families, classes, and severities.
13. CURE-OR v2 confirms formation on 150 registered operational paths: 72
    persistent cliffs and 78 non-cliff controls, with maximum signed
    forward-minus-recovery closure error `1.11e-16` and median cliff endpoint
    risk increase 0.54.
14. The frozen outcome-blind Hybrid25 chart warns 71/72 persistent cliffs,
    raises 3/78 false alarms, and leads by a median of three windows under the
    registered same-benchmark protocol.
15. The calibration-gated anchored update qualifies four of five models,
    removes five unique model–family cliffs, introduces none, and yields mean
    deployment-risk gain 0.06203 with model-seed-clustered 95% interval
    [0.02981, 0.08524].
16. CURE-OR v2 closes formation, warning, and repair with one boundary-transport
    object under a preregistered two-phase execution and preserved outcome-blind
    Phase 1 commitment.
17. The CURE-OR result is prospective over the five registered classifier-head
    seeds and three frozen schedules on the same public benchmark; it is not an
    independent fresh-identity, fresh-dataset, or cross-domain replication.

## Not licensed

1. Covertype does not identify longitudinal sample trajectories, literal first
   crossing times, or synchronized sample crossing because adjacent windows
   contain different observations.
2. The Covertype terminal result is not a complete formal pass. One of ten
   frozen gates fails, and strict robust-median superiority over mean transport
   is not certified.
3. The exact flux identity is bookkeeping. By itself it does not establish a
   universal causal mechanism.
4. Round 11C does not establish a synchronized avalanche; its evidence favors
   persistent distributed crossing.
5. Round 11C is not an installed PyTorch/TorchSig runtime replay. It executes
   the exact tagged signal algorithms used by the frozen configuration through
   a disclosed NumPy runtime.
6. Neither field supplies an externally grounded engineering harm threshold.
   The reported Cliff boundaries are protocol-relative.
7. The result is not universal over neural networks, learners, tasks,
   architectures, shift families, or real RF hardware.
8. TorchSig Round 10's Cliff-aware enrichment uses hidden simulator mechanism
   coordinates. Round 12C removes those coordinates from repair selection, but
   still uses a common queried-label pool and declared path/phase strata. Fully
   unlabelled, automatically discovered deployment coverage remains open.
9. The Covertype signed-margin analysis uses labels and is not an unlabeled
   deployment monitor.
10. A posttarget-selected 45 m Covertype scale is an empirical coherence scale
    for this protocol, not a physical constant.
11. Round 12C does not establish universal superiority over arbitrary random
    sampling. Coverage-random exceeds unstratified random by 0.02734 on average,
    but the five-seed interval spans zero.
12. The 16-cell construction does not isolate which of class, path, or phase is
    individually necessary, and it is not validated outside the two frozen
    TorchSig deployment paths or the ExtraTrees family.
13. Round 13A does not establish a CURE-TSR result. Licensed files were
    unavailable and public metadata did not certify cross-severity identity
    pairing.
14. Round 13B/C are qualification probes, not official corruption-benchmark
    confirmations. The official second-domain claim begins at Round 13D.
15. CIFAR-10-C is a standardized algorithmic corruption benchmark, not natural
    longitudinal deployment drift. Round 13 itself does not test prospective
    warning; that separate function is tested conditionally in CURE-OR.
16. Round 13D/E use three seeds and one SmallCNN family with roughly 66%--68%
    clean accuracy in the mechanism stage. Seed-cluster intervals do not imply
    architectural or task universality.
17. Round 13E is a labelled causal repair experiment, not a standard
    corruption-robustness leaderboard protocol: 2,000 official test identities
    support selection and 8,000 disjoint identities support sealed evaluation.
18. The weighted support-allocation expression involving coverage, boundary
    pressure, deployment mass, redundancy, and transfer is a posttarget
    hypothesis generated by Round 13E. It is not prospectively confirmed.
19. Neither coverage nor hazard concentration is licensed as universally
    optimal. Round 12C identifies a sparse-field coverage regime; Round 13E
    identifies a regime in which extra breadth loses to deeper crossings after
    a broad support floor exists.
20. CURE-OR v2 uses the same public benchmark identities and challenge families;
    it does not license a fresh-data, fresh-identity, cross-dataset, cross-domain,
    or natural longitudinal-drift claim.
21. CURE-OR uses ordered challenge levels, not natural longitudinal deployment
    time. Hybrid25 is outcome blind at deployment, but calibration and repair
    use labels; the complete system is not fully label free.
22. The model seed is the inferential unit (`n = 5`). The 150
    model–schedule–family paths are operational paths, not independent
    replicates, and schedules are not bootstrapped separately.
23. The registered `tau = 0.50` is a benchmark operating boundary, not an
    externally validated engineering safety threshold.
24. The fixed 500-example full-coverage repair confirms risk reduction and
    cliff removal in this domain, but it is not a comparison against pressure,
    hazard, or another allocation policy and does not establish universal
    coverage superiority.
25. Architecture independence applies to the exact paired signed-transport
    accounting identity. The warning and repair results are conditional, and
    CURE-OR v2 does not directly estimate local quantities such as `b`, `H`, or
    an observable-geometry matrix.

## Wording discipline

Use `supports`, `is consistent with`, `confirms within the controlled paired
system`, `second paired neural-network benchmark`, `CURE-OR v2 prospective
same-benchmark confirmation`, `prospective falsification`, and `posttarget
hypothesis`. Do not use `universal`, `absolute safety`, `Covertype sample
synchrony`, `all Covertype gates passed`, `installed TorchSig confirmation`,
`CURE-TSR confirmation`, `fresh-identity CURE-OR confirmation`, or `Round 13E
preregistered confirmation of weighted allocation`.
