# Claims and limits

## Supported

- Exact identity-paired zero–one risk accounting for a fixed deterministic classifier.
- Resolved incident/recovery, turnover, first-crossing and path-conditioned persistence summaries.
- Controlled-field evidence that training support changes anchor risk and later cumulative SBT.
- Conditional warning under declared telemetry, calibration, horizon, event rule and identity anchor.
- Prospective rejection of a universal coverage-first repair rule.

## Not supported

- A universal warning detector or transferable CURE-OR coefficient vector.
- A package-chosen safety boundary.
- Path-independent persistence without a dwell–reversal test.
- Representation-level CURE-OR replication; five heads share one backbone.
- A confirmed replacement law for coverage versus pressure.
- Natural-time or fresh-identity physical validation in the current paper.

Post hoc diagnostic outputs are identified as post hoc and are not relabelled as preregistered confirmation.
